Chat Bridge v0.6.0-beta — the first-run guide

A fresh desk now walks you to your first live conversation, chronologically, then gets out of the way forever.

## What is new

- **"Get your first conversation live"** appears on an empty desk: four steps in order.
  1. Name your profile (type it right there)
  2. Connect a WhatsApp (one button, with the phone-code instructions for remote owners printed under it)
  3. Let the owner pick chats (jumps straight to the consent portal settings)
  4. Open the conversation (the contact appears the moment the owner ticks it)
- Steps flip to done as you actually do them. No timers, no noise: the card refreshes on real events (accounts linking, owners picking, people appearing).
- **It never repeats.** Once your first conversation is visible, or you click the small skip link, the guide is gone for good. Adding accounts two and three is guided by the per-account locked cards, not by a tutorial.
- The visual language matches the desk: dark glass, teal accents, the same card patterns.

## Everything from before still holds

New accounts link locked (the desk sees nothing until the owner picks), the consent portal with remote access for owners in another country, the blindfold enforced on every read, write and open path, the human always sends, official sessions only.

## Install (Windows 10/11)

Download Chat-Bridge-Setup-0.6.0.exe below, double-click, SmartScreen > More info > Run anyway.
